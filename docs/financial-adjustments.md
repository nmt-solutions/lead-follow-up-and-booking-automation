# Refunds and disputes

Operators review an original native captured card charge and its issued seller invoice at `/platform/financial-adjustments`. Business owners and admins see the resulting status, linked documents and in-app notices under their Business. Cancellation remains a separate subscription action.

Provision `platform_operator.refunds` through trusted owner administration. It is a central privileged role and requires current operator MFA. Reconciliation and seller-document privileges remain independent. Issuing a linked credit through this flow requires both refund review and seller-document authority; refund authority does not grant document download, numbering or issuer-configuration rights. The financial feed requires current reconciliation or tax authority.

## Deployment configuration

Web and Trigger use the same native Razorpay account review, credentials and webhook-secret configuration as subscription payments. `FINANCIAL_ADJUSTMENT_POLICY` must contain an explicitly reviewed JSON object with all of these fields:

- `id`, positive `version`, `mode` (`test` or `live`), qualified `account` (`acc_…:test` or `acc_…:live`), `reviewedBy`, `checkedAt`, `effectiveFrom`, `effectiveUntil`, and attributable `evidence` references.
- Explicit booleans `normalRefunds`, `acceptDisputes`, `contestDisputes`, `disputeLostCredit`, and `preserveRecords: true`.
- Each of `partialRefundAccess`, `fullRefundAccess`, `disputeOpenAccess`, `disputeLostAccess`, `disputeWonAccess`, and `disputeClosedAccess` must be `preserve` or `block_period`. The open rule includes bank review. These rules concern the original paid period and its remaining allowance; they create no new grant or paid-through extension.

There is no seeded commercial, legal or access policy. Missing, invalid, future, expired or wrong-account policy blocks new native actions. A used ID/version cannot acquire different contents. An unsent review can be withdrawn and reviewed again under a different approved version. Native reconciliation uses the saved intent even after the action policy is withdrawn; credit issuance still needs current seller-document configuration and authority.

The selected merchant account review must also remain effective immediately before either refund or dispute mutation, including after native evidence reads. An expired review stops a new mutation while retained outcomes remain reconcilable. Review, credit-basis review and dispute-action request keys bind the exact input and original requesting operator. Other currently authorized operators can still inspect, reconcile or initiate their own actions against a case. Dispute evidence edits and retry keys stay with their individual case in the operator form.

Deploy `reconcile-financial-adjustments` with an approved `TRIGGER_FINANCIAL_ADJUSTMENTS_CRON`. The Trigger worker reads saved native identities and commits results; it cannot create a new refund or issue a seller document. Configure `/api/webhooks/razorpay-adjustments` for enabled refund created/processed/failed and dispute created/under-review/action-required/won/lost/closed events. Raw-body HMAC, merchant identity and event ID are checked before an append-only receipt commits. The endpoint acknowledges durable receipt without calling the provider. Trigger later retrieves authoritative native state. Unmatched receipts remain in the operator queue for attributable review.

## Review and recovery

1. Issue the seller invoice for a genuinely confirmed paid charge. Select original net line amounts and retain the private reason/evidence references. The original selected-line cumulative allocator supplies the exact gross amount and original tax components. Existing credits and outstanding cases reserve their portions. Review does not move money.
2. Request the exact normal refund after reviewing its currency, original payment/invoice and policy. The immutable intent includes its native `X-Refund-Idempotency` key, receipt, notes and complete serialized request body before I/O. Normal speed is explicit. Native payment customer, invoice, order, mode/account, captured state, currency, amount and remaining refundable amount are checked independently; current authority is checked immediately before the POST. Pending/unknown partials serialize further native refund requests for that payment.
3. Pending, processed and failed are distinct native results. Creation time is not processing time or bank-settlement proof. An unknown acknowledgement retains its original reservation. Recovery follows the saved native ID or paged payment-scoped receipt/notes lookup. An absent page never authorizes another POST. This implementation does not assume an undocumented idempotency retention period and does not automatically repeat an ambiguous mutation. A positively proven preflight stop before the mutation endpoint may resume the same saved refund key/body after current authority is restored.
4. Review an existing dispute by its exact native ID and original charge. Reconciliation validates its payment, currency, amount, phase, response deadline, status and actual deduction. Accept uses the native accept endpoint. Evidence draft and submit use the native contest endpoint and remain separate outcomes. Submit requires evidence; provider submission is not a win. There is no generic dispute idempotency header. A saved unknown action is reconciled with the original dispute and exact submitted evidence; it is never blindly replayed.
5. For evidence, select native documents already uploaded to the selected merchant and reviewed for this payment. The authenticated `/documents/:id/content` response must match the selected SHA-256 and supported PDF/JPEG/PNG content type/signature. The application limit is 2 MiB per document and five documents per action. Exact private bytes and the approved body commit before mutation. Retained evidence downloads require current refund authority/MFA. Arbitrary URLs and unchecked document IDs are not supported. The text seller artifact is not relabeled or automatically uploaded as a PDF.
6. A processed refund or lost-dispute deduction produces an append-only financial effect independently of later credit work. Original tax/line basis, actual amount and source identity are available through the narrow financial feed for subsequent bank/tax work. They do not assert settlement or tax filing. A changed terminal result or conflicting deduction retains the first durable fact and requires review; a later valid native read clears the review condition. A signed action-required receipt plus the current native open state can reopen evidence work without treating an old event as authority.
7. Issue the linked credit with current seller-document authority and configuration. Numbering, source, request and adjustment identities reuse #48's stable allocator. Native reservations prevent unrelated manual credits from consuming the selected basis. Recognized native case IDs require the exact confirmed source, lines and canonical credit intent. The original paid period, invoice, tax rules and usage are never rewritten. If the actual deduction differs, use **Review or correct the credit basis** with current refund and document authority. Select original net lines and retain explicit approval rationale/evidence. The original cumulative allocator computes the credit, capped by the actual native money. The append-only review retains any uncredited difference for later reconciliation; it does not classify that difference as a fee or a tax exemption. Review the resulting exact amount before separately issuing the permanent numbered document. A basis cannot change after a note is issued. Actual deductions also constrain later money requests independently of document rounding. Document artifact failure remains recoverable under its original number and never repeats the money request.

All financial intents, private evidence, native receipts and financial/access events are protected history. The three new ledgers have forced RLS and append-only runtime grants. Business summaries use a scoped, redacted reader; no private evidence or recovery token is returned. Managed schema reapplication pauses writes and reconciles the scoped FKs dependent on replaceable unique constraints. Native receipt timestamps come from the database. No deletion or retention override is introduced.

If an earlier reservation is withdrawn, fails or receives a different reviewed credit basis, the later native refund amount remains frozen. Before consuming a document number, the serialized seller-document transaction checks the computed original-tax allocation against the saved financial review. A changed allocation requires a fresh attributable credit-basis review; the incurred refund and any residual stay recorded without another native request. Credit review allocates against actual issued notes and earlier reservations while keeping every outstanding reservation within the original net-line limits.

Customer notices are actual persisted `in_app / available` effects with stable event IDs. Availability does not establish reading, email acceptance, email delivery or bank receipt. Final #59 commit `7318cebb98ec49a70aa4014eb58b9cfafa104afa` is merged, including its immutable source identity and cross-account retry guard. Its subscription preferences now include billing/refund/dispute notices for current Business owners/admins. The existing worker selects the latest substantive financial notice for each case, rechecks the self-only preference and current Business authority, seals the exact payload, and uses the actual configured Resend sender. Superseded financial stages are not newly emailed. The destination is the Business's financial history. Email acceptance and signed delivery history remain in #59's notification records; no financial action is repeated to produce email. Missing Resend setup or absent opt-in leaves the in-app effect intact. Operator reconciliation/document/email privileges remain separate; this composition does not grant a new operator email audience.
