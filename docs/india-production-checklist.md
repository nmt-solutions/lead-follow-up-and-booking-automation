# India production release: owner checklist

Updated 13 November 2026. Launch scope: Indian billing addresses, monthly INR subscriptions through Razorpay. International checkout remains unavailable until the Merchant of Record is selected, approved and integrated. Razorpay's existing international merchant activation can remain enabled; it does not open international purchases in Lead Relay.

## Already prepared

- NMT's supplied company, GSTIN, registered address, support contact and designated grievance officer are published.
- The immutable India price catalog, approved standard domestic GST rules and the compatible invoice issuer are present in the connected database. Regular GST and the supplied e-invoice turnover declaration have already been recorded; do not request those declarations again.
- Signup, email verification, authenticator setup, optional mailbox/calendar onboarding, plan comparison, scoped credits, payment reconciliation, invoices and subscription cancellation are implemented.
- Production checkout checks Indian billing country, INR, the published monthly offer, live account settings, unambiguous native plan mappings, signed-webhook configuration, cancellation/refund policies, invoice protection and worker configuration. Advanced checkout and pilot paths cannot bypass these checks. Existing payment events and financial history remain recoverable.
- A Business also needs its approved India discovery configuration and an active AI profile before checkout opens. These checks do not grant discovery rights or approve providers.

## Required before taking customer payments

1. **Configure live Razorpay in Vercel and the workers.** Your merchant is already active; the available environment still contains test integration settings. Set live `RAZORPAY_KEY_ID`, `RAZORPAY_KEY_SECRET`, `RAZORPAY_WEBHOOK_SECRETS` and an accurate `RAZORPAY_ACCOUNT_REVIEW` containing actual live account/plan references and retained account evidence. This release uses the reviewed recurring-card path. Do not enable eMandate or attach an unreviewed UPI path to that card configuration.

2. **Create three native Razorpay plans, then map their IDs.** Use monthly billing, interval `1`, INR and no reserved-capacity ID. With the configured standard 18% GST, the collection totals are Starter **₹2,358.82** (`235882` paise), Growth **₹5,898.82** (`589882` paise) and Scale **₹11,798.82** (`1179882` paise). Their subscription prices before GST remain ₹1,999 / ₹4,999 / ₹9,999. Map exactly one native plan per public offer (`starter`, `growth`, `scale`) in the live review. The app creates each customer's subscription; you do not create customer subscriptions manually. Native plan amount/currency/interval are checked again before checkout. [Razorpay plan API](https://razorpay.com/docs/api/payments/subscriptions/create-plan).

3. **Configure live signed webhooks and operating policies.** Use `https://leadrelay.nmtsolutions.in/api/payment-events` for subscription/payment lifecycle events and `https://leadrelay.nmtsolutions.in/api/webhooks/razorpay-adjustments` for refund/dispute events. Add the configured secret(s) to the server's webhook key ring; never put them in client variables or the repository. Supply live, current `SUBSCRIPTION_LIFECYCLE_POLICY` (cycle-end cancellation, paid-through access, protected records) and `FINANCIAL_ADJUSTMENT_POLICY` for the same live account. Test delivery, signature validation and duplicate handling. [Razorpay webhook validation](https://razorpay.com/docs/webhooks/validate-test).

4. **Enable Trigger organization deployments and complete deployment.** The earlier upload was rejected with “Deployments are not available for this organization.” A deployment is not established by a project reference or API key. After entitlement/access is resolved, deploy, inspect the actual task/schedules and run the read-only probe using the commands in [production launch](production-launch.md). Record `TRIGGER_PRODUCTION_DEPLOYMENT_VERSION` only after the promoted worker and probe pass. Web and workers need the same approved configuration, including payment, notification, invoice and refund recovery schedules.

5. **Approve and fund the discovery/AI service.** Supply the genuine Exa scope/data-use rights and limits, supported funded AI configuration and approved profile, and deployed pooled capacity. Provision an approved policy for each customer Business with India coverage and the exact `discovery credits` unit; its reviewed public offering and buyer categories must fit that customer. The Business must explicitly include India in its confirmed search areas. This is currently operator provisioning, not automatic provisioning of arbitrary customer products. Use [discovery setup](discovery.md) and [capacity setup](discovery-capacity.md). Customer subscription payment does not establish source rights or enable outreach.

6. **Verify email, storage and optional connections.** Verify Resend's production sender/domain, authentication DNS, event webhook and notification delivery. Protect the invoice/credit-note R2 prefix from expiry and confirm the retention setting only after checking the bucket policy. Complete Google/Microsoft production OAuth verification and fixed callbacks if you want customers to connect Gmail/Outlook/calendars at launch; connections stay optional and unavailable providers remain disabled. See [email connection setup](email-connections-setup.md). Arrange routine GST return filing/accounting from the retained invoices and credit notes; app configuration does not file returns.

7. **Complete a controlled acceptance run.** Use a fresh release-test Business and an explicitly authorized payment. Check signup/MFA, a first search, the exact GST-inclusive total, verified capture before access, invoice download, renewal/failure recovery, cancellation preserving paid-through access, and refund/credit-note reconciliation. Verify a teammate and another Business cannot read private financial records. UI acceptance and actual live payment/renewal tests remain owner-owned; no real customer has been charged for this release.
