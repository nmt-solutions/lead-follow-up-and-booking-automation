# Use Razorpay for direct subscriptions

Use Razorpay as the initial payment gateway for direct INR subscriptions in India and USD subscriptions internationally, keeping billing/tax management and entitlements in our app. Its documented international-card recurring/proration path fits the approved upgrade policy; start with cards and add UPI AutoPay after validating its separate upgrade/new-mandate workflow, while preserving domestic-card restrictions. Cashfree remains a researched alternative if merchant approval or commercial terms are unsuitable; actual international activation, subscription surcharge and FX terms must be confirmed before live billing.
