# Use Paddle as Merchant of Record

For an India-based SaaS serving international Businesses, select Paddle as Merchant of Record rather than initially owning the direct-processor sales-tax and buyer-invoicing workflow. Accept its transaction fees and contractual obligations in exchange for its contracted billing and transaction-tax administration, while retaining application usage accounting, product delivery, support, privacy, and our own business accounting/taxes. Product/merchant acceptance and payout verification remain prerequisites to activation; changing billing providers after subscriptions exist carries migration cost.
