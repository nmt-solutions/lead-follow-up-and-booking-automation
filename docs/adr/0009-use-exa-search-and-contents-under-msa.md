# Use Exa Search and Contents under an MSA

Select Exa Search (Auto by default, Lite for suitable focused lookups) and Contents behind a model-independent retrieval adapter for prospect discovery and source verification. Use the MSA/executed-order route with explicit durable lead/evidence retention, customer display/export and post-cancellation scope, rather than infer the full product's rights from general self-serve terms. This finalizes the provider and contract route over the reviewed alternatives' unresolved or restrictive output terms; the actual order, data permissions and commercial quote remain unexecuted production prerequisites, recorded in [the decision](../search-provider-decision.md).
