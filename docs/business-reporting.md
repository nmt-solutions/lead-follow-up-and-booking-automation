# Discovery quality and sales reporting

`/businesses/{businessId}/reports` and GET `/api/businesses/{businessId}/reports` read the same application query. Owners, Admins and Salespeople use current `sales.work` membership and persisted session/MFA checks. Scope and assurance are checked before collecting any data and again before returning it. The query performs no provider I/O, export, commercial command, billing mutation or durable job.

## Time, coverage and identities

The query accepts `from`, `to`, `category`, `offset` and `limit` (1–50). Defaults are the preceding 30 days through the real configured clock. Both endpoints are inclusive, so adjacent intervals can share boundary events. Future end times and reversed/empty intervals are rejected. `asOf` is generation time, not a caller-selected historical snapshot.

Every measure supplies its source-time or cohort definition. Current status, corrections and request expiry are evaluated at generation time, including evidence newer than the selected interval. Retained historical events remain distinct. Changing paid reach, cancelling or stopping acquisition does not erase historical outcomes or require current acquisition capacity to read this report.

All aggregate source queries include the entire retained Business population; no workspace/run/detail page limit is used to produce counts. Detail pagination happens after collection, with exact total and next offset. Pages reauthorize and regenerate. Concurrent corrections can move entries; pages are not an immutable cross-request snapshot. Earliest retained source times are displayed, with an explicit unavailable report-wide retention cutoff. They are not completeness guarantees. Capture is limited to selected supported/imported channels. Ignored/unsupported/uncaptured traffic and expired optional source artifacts must not be interpreted as zero activity.

Canonical discovery prospect identities represent a prospect and its purchasing need, not necessarily a unique person or company. Buyer identities come from explicit current conversation associations. One buyer can have several Opportunities, and the same need can span channels. No name/domain/phone heuristic combines them. Reassociation changes current engagement and qualification; immutable source and association events remain inspectable.

Denominators of zero produce a null rate, never a fabricated percentage. Event counts without a shared population do not produce conversion rates. Outcomes legitimately overlap for a purchasing need; the UI does not present these independent measures as a single funnel.

Native WhatsApp manual activity is reported separately from the general manual-reply ledger. It requires a real authenticated app-echo identity bound to an applied manual-control source event; application-origin echoes, status events and buyer requests for a person do not qualify. Its interval uses the control event's stable recorded time, which survives private artifact expiry. Response coverage uses the native occurrence time only while that timing evidence is retained. Current next-action details cover all retained Opportunities, including unengaged prospects, independently of the event interval.

WhatsApp permission-withdrawal command events are separate from captured email opt-outs. Native ledger identity makes exact replay one event, without guessing unique recipients or inferring permission changes from arbitrary incoming text. The original permission scope and its Owner/Admin evidence workflow remain authoritative.

## Costs, permissions and provenance

Discovery allowance reservations and unused authorized run budgets are grouped by their recorded unit. They are capacity, not money or current subscription entitlement. Provider-returned Exa USD estimates are a subtotal with explicit operation coverage, separate from unavailable actual invoice charges and AI expenses. Missing estimates are unknown, not free. No planning exchange rate becomes an actual expense.

Meta usage counts distinct reserved intents with exact retained authenticated account/generation/peer/native-message/callback binding and native pricing classification. Classification is observed-unpriced. This report has no actual customer Meta statement charge; it does not assume zero or add customer-paid usage to the platform allowance price or operator expense. Global operator setup/support/sales effort and other operator costs are never queried or exposed. The report does not grant billing or integration capabilities to Salespeople.

Details contain scoped source IDs, versions, times, controlled descriptions and application links. They omit native callback/lease capabilities, credentials, account assets, private envelopes, provider payloads and privileged billing data. Original evidence, commercial authority and actor attribution stay in the existing authorized source workflows. An RFQ created outside the interval can still contribute an in-interval revision to detail history.

Issue #56 exports retain their independent Owner/Admin, current MFA, effective policy, source-contract rights, actor binding and expiry checks. Report access grants no export/download/cleanup authority. Offboarding and cancellation preserve canonical financial/domain history; source-permitted reporting remains readable. This query neither invokes offboarding nor bypasses its acquisition fences.

## Validation and UI acceptance

Application tests use actual restricted PostgreSQL and real setup/intake/association/RFQ/commercial/appointment/call/control workflows, deterministic providers and a controlled clock. Coverage includes repeated discovery/replay, clock expiry, native statuses, cross-channel qualification, scoped opt-outs, corrected current state versus immutable history, role/session denial, complete paginated detail and populated managed schema reapplication.

The source flow applies both required Apple design skills: quiet semantic Tailwind/shadcn surfaces, persistent UTC labels, 44px controls, Radix tabs, nearby loading/error/status, authentication recovery, expandable definitions and source identities, wrapping content and preserved filters. Source review follows `layout.md > Visual hierarchy` (“Use progressive disclosure”) and `feedback.md > Best practices` (“Consider integrating status feedback into your interface”). No new motion/material system is introduced. Browser rendering, measured contrast, keyboard/zoom/viewport/appearance/preference checks and user acceptance remain pending.
