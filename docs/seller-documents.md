# Numbered seller invoices and credit notes

Issue #48 implements app-owned seller documents for the direct-selling decision. A confirmed `subscription_period` is the source. Customer amounts, quotes, tax previews and gateway receipts cannot authorize an invoice. The document uses the period's **actual tax lines and charge basis**, independently of provider, root subscription ID, initial/pilot quote lines or current catalog prices. Each captured period/payment receives its own invoice. Native initial and renewal invoices preserve the complete period-specific `charge` observation, including provider invoice/order/payment/subscription/plan, dates and separate fee/base-currency evidence. Later reconciliation of the subscription cannot replace that history.

## Configuration and authority

A separately provisioned `platform_operator.documents` capability and current operator MFA authorize issuer configuration, approval, global document review and credit notes. The capability participates in central authentication/MFA classification. Business Owners/Admins may request an invoice for an own-Business confirmed payment, review history, recover its artifact and download it. Salespeople and foreign Businesses have no financial access. Customer declarations and request flags cannot approve configuration. Every operation checks current persisted authority; downloads repeat that check after storage I/O.

Native Razorpay `test` payments require fixture tax and issuer configuration; their original payment mode stays `test` in frozen evidence and the readable artifact/history explicitly identify the fixture document. Server composition selects this fixture path only for an explicitly configured test gateway. Live issuance and credits require live payment, tax and issuer evidence, including on idempotent replay. Changing execution mode never turns a sandbox payment into a live sale. Existing authorized history/downloads remain available independently of the current gateway's readiness.

Open **Businesses → Review seller documents → Configure and review the seller issuer**. Save a complete draft with explicit fixture/live mode, effective interval, seller legal identity, applicable legal fields, required customer fields, two distinct numbering series, supported currency precision and original tax rounding, template titles/footer, retention policy and checked review evidence. Approve the exact saved version with a recorded reason. No production legal requirement, identity, rate, price, number or retention period is seeded. These records are attributable operator assertions; the application does not establish legal sufficiency or fetch evidence references.

The highest decided version of the selected mode effective at the paid period's start determines invoice configuration. Future versions wait; an expired or withdrawn successor does not revive older coverage. Credits select a current effective approved issuer version for their own numbering and wording, retaining the original issuer, customer and financial basis separately. Drafts do not authorize issuance. A reused series key keeps the same prefix, first number and width across versions; use a new key/prefix to establish another reviewed series. Current INR/USD financial precision is two decimal places, explicitly selected in configuration. Each currency has a primary rounding policy and an explicit list of additional approved policies for independently rounded charge lines. Each original line must match a reviewed policy; invoice issuance never rounds or reprices a captured charge again.

## Immutable issuance and adjustments

PostgreSQL serializes issuer/number allocation with one transaction advisory lock. Database uniqueness covers displayed number, series ordinal, period invoice identity, request key, adjustment identity and original/source adjustment identity. Every replay receives current authorization. Identical payloads reuse the same identity; changed payload/key reuse conflicts. Alternate request keys for the same payment or adjustment are durably recorded against the existing document. An artifact failure never frees its number.

The issued snapshot preserves the original reviewed customer and evidence hashes, quote/pricing, actual tax calculation/rules/approvals/registrations/evidence, amounts, currency, rounding, provider/account/payment/period, approved seller configuration and attribution. Runtime privileges prohibit rewriting these columns or deleting history. Corrections append numbered credit notes with a same-Business original invoice and paid period, exact payment reference, actor/time, reason, evidence and stable adjustment/source identity.

`adjust` is the narrow idempotent command for #49 to consume after its own refund/dispute authorization. Input identifies the original invoice, period/payment, adjustment ID, stable manual/refund/dispute source and positive **net minor-unit amounts for selected original line IDs**. The service derives all tax and gross amounts; it performs no refund/provider action. Duplicate source records cannot create another credit. All pending/failed-artifact credits count toward cumulative financial bounds.

The reviewed `cumulative_original_components_v1` policy apportions each original line's already-allocated tax components in proportion to cumulative credited net. It applies that original rule's rounding mode/increment, subtracts previously credited components, caps each component and preserves the exact final remainder at full credit. It never applies today's rates or reallocates tax from an unrelated line. The original rounding scope and subtotal allocation remain frozen; cumulative rounding and final remainders are explicit in each credit snapshot. Selected lines cannot exceed their original remaining net or tax allocation. Credit amounts are positive reductions, clearly labeled as credits rather than signed new charges.

## Private artifact lifecycle

The reviewed `text_v1` format produces a readable UTF-8 `.txt` attachment with seller/customer legal fields, numbered document/date, payment/period, line net/tax/gross, original tax rules and rounding, evidence, totals, configuration and correction attribution. It is a usable document, not raw JSON. This implementation does not generate PDF and makes no PDF render/visual verification claim. If a supported jurisdiction needs a different format or additional legal wording, configure that wording or implement and approve a new template version before activation. Keep the renderer of an existing template version immutable.

Artifacts reuse the existing private US R2 adapter, bounded byte policy, account/bucket fingerprint, conditional `IfNoneMatch: *` writes and Business/document/owner/SHA-256 metadata. The immutable numbered intent and exact byte/hash identity commit before any storage effect. Outbound put/get attempts commit before I/O and remain visible as uncertain until confirmed. Lost acknowledgements and precondition conflicts reconcile by reading and validating the exact bytes, hash and ownership metadata. A conflicting object remains failed; the application never overwrites it or allocates another number. Application usage is not provider billing.

Issued artifacts have dedicated protected ownership in `seller_document` and the `seller-documents/<Business>/<document>` namespace. They are **not** generic `business_file` uploads. Generic billing delete/expiry/cleanup/purge cannot select their ledger or keys. The configured minimum retention is a floor, not an automatic deletion/access deadline; `protected_history` retains the artifact and ledger beyond it. No financial deletion/offboarding command is supplied. An eventual legally approved disposition workflow must explicitly account for this history; ordinary file retention is insufficient.

Configure the existing private-file/R2 environment and explicitly set `SELLER_DOCUMENT_STORAGE_PROTECTED_CONFIRMED=true` only after checking the `seller-documents/` prefix is protected from expiring bucket lifecycle, bulk deletion and offboarding policies for the approved retention requirement. Consider the provider's actual bucket-lock controls as appropriate to the reviewed policy; the application does not install or verify those controls. No public `r2.dev` endpoint, public custom domain or direct object/presigned URL is used.

Downloads require a persisted hashed actor-bound expiring grant in `x-file-token` plus the current authenticated session and current Owner/Admin or document-operator authority. Tokens never enter URLs. The application validates storage ownership/type/size/hash and rechecks grants/session/role/MFA after I/O. Responses are private, no-store attachments with sandbox/nosniff/no-referrer headers. Delivered copies cannot be recalled.

Trigger owns durable artifact recovery through `recover-seller-documents`. Set the actual `TRIGGER_SELLER_DOCUMENT_RECOVERY_CRON`, deploy the task and verify its attached schedule with the same restricted DB/R2 configuration. No cadence is seeded. A bounded database claim leases only pending/failed intents in the configured account scope, using database time; its narrow output contains identifiers and a recovery capability. Scoped worker access expires against database time, cannot issue new documents/credits or create download grants, and does not impersonate the purchaser. Manual authorized recovery remains available. Review unresolved failures and unconfirmed storage attempts in history.

## Interfaces and database application

- Own Business: `/businesses/:id/seller-documents`, GET/POST `/api/businesses/:id/seller-documents`, POST `.../recover`, POST `.../:documentId/access`, GET `.../:documentId/content`.
- Document operator: `/platform/seller-documents`, `.../configuration`, `.../:businessId`; corresponding `/api/platform/seller-documents` queue/configuration/decision and Business endpoints. POST `.../:businessId/credits` records an adjustment. Operator selection is route-owned, never a request-body flag.

The additive schema introduces six forced-RLS tables: `seller_issuer_revision`, `seller_issuer_decision`, `seller_document`, `seller_document_request`, `seller_document_access`, `seller_document_storage`, and the default-false operator capability. Financial/configuration/request/grant columns are append-only. Only artifact execution state/failure and storage outcome have narrow runtime UPDATE grants. Startup checks SELECT and INSERT separately, every immutable column, policies, five validated composite scopes and three fixed-search-path SECURITY DEFINER helper protections. Prepare revokes table/column access and helper execution before removing managed scope FKs; secure restores/validates them and grants after push.

The parent merger must apply `pnpm db-push` using the existing **ROOT** owner environment, then verify the actual restricted runtime and retained target data. The implementer only applies disposable PostgreSQL schemas; no target rollout, migration files, production seed, credentials, live payment or provider activation is implied.

## Verification and acceptance

Tests use public application commands/queries/HTTP with real restricted PostgreSQL, genuine reviewed purchases/configuration, deterministic conditional private storage/gateway and a controlled clock. They cover configuration/MFA, authoritative financial sources, concurrency/replays, version changes with unchanged originals, original tax credit allocation and cumulative caps, storage ambiguity/corruption/recovery, protected cleanup, scope/current-role/grant revocation, safe attachments/errors and actual schema reapplication with replaceable UNIQUE constraints and dependent scopes. An operational paid fixture alone is rejected as invoice authority.

Both required frontend design skills apply to configuration → review → issuance → history → artifact recovery/download → original invoice → credit review/confirmation. The UI uses the shared Tailwind/shadcn theme, labeled controls, textual states, wrapping opaque record surfaces, progressive evidence disclosure and preserved entries through authentication errors. Source/flow review does not establish browser, visual, contrast, keyboard, zoom or human acceptance; those remain pending with the user.
