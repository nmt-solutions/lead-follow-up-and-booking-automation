# Guarded customer-owned WhatsApp messages

Issue #40 adds native Cloud API text and approved body-only positional text-template transport. It composes the existing guarded Opportunity dispatch for both persisted grounded inquiry replies and Owner-approved, bounded prospect campaigns. It uses conventional TypeScript and real restricted PostgreSQL; Trigger owns recovery. It neither purchases Meta credit nor bills Meta messaging through our subscription.

## Setup and supported scope

Complete [customer-owned onboarding](whatsapp-onboarding.md), the exact-account direct-payment review where needed, and [signed intake](whatsapp-intake.md). Current Meta ownership, required grants, registered number, account review, direct payment, incoming capture and supported manual activity remain independent conditions. The implemented sending path requires observed supported Business App coexistence activity; a Cloud-only number without supported manual visibility remains visibly blocked. This is a limited supported path, not a claim of universal device or outgoing-history coverage. Unsupported media, buttons, named template parameters, authentication templates and financial promises in prospect templates remain explicit gates.

Deploy `recover-whatsapp-sends` and then confirm `META_OUTBOUND_WORKER_DEPLOYMENT_CONFIRMED=true` for that actual deployment. The web and worker use the same native Meta configuration, encrypted customer authorization and restricted database setup. There is no fixture transport or alternate provider fallback in production. Apply the reviewed integration schema with the existing `pnpm db-push` environment; do not copy credentials between checkouts.

Business → WhatsApp identity → **Review WhatsApp permissions and messages** opens `/businesses/{businessId}/whatsapp/sending`. Native sending administration and automatic ordinary-inquiry evaluation/dispatch require a current Owner/Admin and MFA. Salespeople retain shared inbox, takeover and manual handling access; this path does not grant them native integration access. The workspace distinguishes account sending capability, paid automation allowance, individual action eligibility and provider evidence. Account readiness does not grant permission to a recipient.

## Recipient permission and ordinary replies

Record an attributable opt-in for the exact Business, recipient, purpose and optionally purchasing need, including supplied-number confirmation, reviewed notices, a redacted case reference/digest, observation time and explicit validity. Keep actual evidence in the approved archive; never enter tokens or payment credentials. Public phone availability, paid discovery reach and Meta approving a template are separate facts. Revocation and evidenced opt-out block future transport, including duplicate records of withdrawn evidence within the affected scope. Overlapping permission after withdrawal needs a later observation and a distinct evidence digest; relabeling/re-dating the revoked artifact cannot restore it. Changed permission needs fresh attributable evidence, not an email-policy override.

Ordinary replies begin in the existing Opportunity **Answer and qualify** flow. The recipient, parent reference and body come from its persisted current inquiry action and grounded answer. The caller cannot supply replacement text or recipients. Within the customer service window, the native adapter sends that exact text with its native parent. The window is computed from authenticated incoming message occurrence times, never webhook arrival, processing time or replay. An old delayed message does not reopen it.

Outside the window, first retrieve an approved template in the exact language and with explicit positional values. Bind it to the pending reply only when the complete rendering equals the saved grounded answer. A mismatching template cannot authorize a different answer. Every template send fetches current native status/components again and compares its ID, language, category, content, parameter values and rendering. A paused, replaced or unsupported template blocks transport.

## Bounded prospect outreach

Choose current sourced phone contacts from Discovery. Save a campaign version with its fixed recipients and source/contact snapshots, customer-owned sender/assets/generation, exact rendered templates, current Commercial authority version, local weekday/time window, earliest start, expiry, explicit delays, attempt cap and mandatory stops. Supported informational messages do not require enabling price automation. Meta template approval and first-outreach approval do not authorize a price, discount, quotation or order commitment.

The Owner separately approves the immutable version and its complete hash. Starting execution is a distinct command. New discoveries never expand the fixed set; changed recipients, sender, messages, parameters or timing require a new version and approval. A later version fences older work. Current source evidence, paid reach, original approver authority/session and Commercial authority are checked again during dispatch.

Each step is one durable Opportunity action. A follow-up waits for its minimum delay, approved local window and authoritative Sent-equivalent evidence for its preceding step. Missing/ambiguous outcomes do not authorize another attempt. Replies, opt-out, takeover, changed associations/authority, failed setup and expiry stop affected work. Handback permits a new evaluation; it never revives cancelled actions. Trigger rediscovers due actions after restart, without message contents in job arguments. Bounded recovery batches persist each action's last worker visit and prioritize older unvisited work; an unresolved or deferred action cannot monopolize subsequent batches. Worker-visit bookkeeping grants no transport authority.

## Side effects and authenticated recovery

The shared Business lock serializes dispatch with policy/control changes. The action and immutable exact intent are committed before any send, including a cryptographically random private provider correlation reference. A single invocation owns the pending→committed transition. Native transport makes one bounded `POST /{phone-number-id}/messages`; it supplies no invented provider idempotency header and performs no internal retry. `biz_opaque_callback_data` carries the committed private reference, which is omitted from client responses. A copyable public action UUID cannot establish acceptance. The reference is correlation, not a provider deduplication guarantee.

Account credential inspection and template reads can consume time or reveal lost access. All current authority, paid reservation, control, inquiry, permission, visibility, account and window conditions repeat after that I/O immediately before transport. Accepted responses preserve the native message ID separately from delivery. Timeout, ambiguous response, process loss or result-write failure leave the committed intent reserved, with no automatic resend.

Reconciliation consumes #39's exact-raw-HMAC authenticated, durable account-bound status receipts. It requires the selected connection/generation, WABA/number, recipient, appropriate occurrence/receipt time and exact action callback or an already-known native message reference. Unknown origin/unsupported status fields, conflicting identities, conflicting outcomes and missing evidence remain unresolved with reasons. A marker alone establishes no manual or application-origin message. Application echo classification additionally requires the persisted exact accepted native reference, peer and body. Ordinary outgoing reply references resolve through the current purchasing-need association; a prospect reply can establish engagement only through its actual received record and the protected exact native reference chain.

Statuses are separate from human activity. Pending/gap intake retains #39's exact need/peer fencing, including immutable scope after staging expiry. Unrelated needs and peers remain executable. Reconciliation may establish accepted, Sent, delivered, read or failed; none is a quotation, booking, buying interest or sale. No supported GET-message-history/status endpoint is invented. If authoritative callbacks never arrive, uncertainty remains visible.

## Allowance and customer Meta usage

Both ordinary and prospect attempts consume the effective #44 paid subscription's `provider calls` automation unit through the existing shared reservation. The same reservation is rechecked at the current transport boundary; unknown acceptance stays consumed. Zero, expired or mismatched units block automation, while incoming and stored work remain available. No independent WhatsApp quota or unpaid live bypass is introduced.

Usage is grouped by immutable action/native message identity. Duplicate status callbacks do not add another message or paid reservation. Provider usage classifications retain authenticated provenance and provider-observed versus received timestamps. A classification without an actual monetary amount remains **observed unpriced**, including when a provider billable flag is false; no rate or zero-price conclusion is inferred. Assisted case-backed currency/minor-unit estimates are recorded separately and never overwrite provider observations. Meta's final account bill remains external. These records create neither a subscription invoice line nor an operator-spend charge.

## Database and live limits

The append-only `whatsapp_outbound` ledger contains permission/revocation, verified templates, immutable plans/decisions, exact intents/results and estimates. It has forced RLS, separate SELECT and INSERT verification, no runtime UPDATE/DELETE, exact connection/Business foreign keys and scoped unique identities. Opportunity actions add immutable nullable prospect-plan origin fields and a separately mutable `meta_recovery_at` worker-visit timestamp; existing ordinary origins remain constrained. Receipts add immutable minimal status evidence that survives private staging expiry. The protected prospect→inquiry transition requires the exact accepted outgoing and authenticated incoming reference chain. `pending_meta_outbound()` is a narrowly scoped fixed-search-path definer discovery function; PUBLIC cannot execute it and it returns no credentials/content.

Actual customer account activation, Meta Tech Provider/App Review/live/coexistence approvals, correct callback/field subscriptions, direct-payment evidence, supported device coverage, deployed workers, retained opt-in/evidence/data policies and actual paid scope remain live inputs. No real customer message, browser validation or production Meta account validation is claimed. Browser/UI and user acceptance remain pending.
