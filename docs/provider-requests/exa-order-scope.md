# Exa order-form scope

Prepared for the selected Exa Search + Contents integration. **Draft only: not sent, accepted or executed.** Fill the operator's actual legal entity and billing details during procurement; no identity or budget commitment is inferred here.

## Product and end users

An India-based operator provides a multi-tenant SaaS to subscribing Businesses across industries. The application researches public potential buyers, relevant public professional/business contacts and explicit buying requests, then supports qualification, sales conversations and customer-approved actions. It is not resale of an Exa API key, raw API access or a general search engine.

Please include this Customer Product and its subscribed Business end users within the current Exa MSA/Order Form. The application will retain its own Business-scoped records and provide research, evidence review, conversation handling and workflow value.

## Required scope

Confirm the right for the operator and subscribed Businesses to:

1. Use Search and Contents API results to create and update derived company, purchasing-role and public work-contact facts, matching reasons, source URLs/check dates, buying-request status and short evidence excerpts.
2. Cache/store those records durably in the SaaS database, display them to the authorized subscribing Business and its team, and export the Business's records. State any TTL, attribution, deletion or field restrictions.
3. Continue retaining and using permitted derived records/evidence and customer exports after the Exa subscription/order ends, while API access and Exa confidential materials remain governed separately. State any required deletion exceptions explicitly.
4. Use the records in the described discovery/CRM workflow, including user-initiated first-email handoff and bounded approved messaging subject to applicable recipient permission, identity, opt-out and channel rules. Confirm the scope does not constitute prohibited resale, a competing search service or an unapproved data-broker product.
5. Treat third-party source/publisher rights and applicable privacy obligations separately; the agreement must not be represented as ownership of every original web page or universal outreach permission.

## Input and data handling

Initial queries use minimal public company/product/role/geography information, not private mailbox messages, internal catalogues/prices, credentials or identified-person inputs. Results can include publicly listed professional/business contact information. Confirm the applicable DPA, processing roles, permitted input/output fields, retention, improvement/training purposes and subprocessors for that exact path.

State any no-training/ZDR options and their cost/scope. Do not assume the public DPA itself prohibits all improvement/training, or that every Exa product has identical ZDR behavior. Any expansion into private/customer-person data requires an appropriate agreed scope before use.

## Commercial request

- Initial volume: 1-10 Business pilots, controlled by application per-run and operator-wide budgets. Provide the actual quote rather than assume these are contracted traffic commitments.
- Target the published usage model without a fixed minimum if available: Lite USD 4/1,000 requests, Auto USD 7/1,000 and Contents USD 1/1,000 page/content-type units. Confirm result counts, extra results, summaries, cache/freshness charges, rejected/failed request billing and rounding.
- State every monthly/annual minimum, prepayment, auto-reload, overage, support/ZDR charge, payment-method requirement, tax/withholding provision and termination effect. No unquoted Enterprise purchase is authorized by this draft.
- The executed order should identify the governing MSA/DPA versions and precedence over conflicting general terms. Preserve the applicable contractual scope with our integration records.
