### Merchant of Record versus direct processor

With a Merchant of Record, the buyer purchases from the reseller/legal seller, which handles the contracted checkout, buyer invoicing, applicable transaction-tax collection/remittance, and billing/refund/dispute administration before paying our net proceeds. Refunds, chargebacks, and associated costs can still be recovered from us; operational handling is not a guarantee against financial loss. We retain product delivery, technical support, privacy, and our business's own accounting/tax responsibilities. [Paddle agreement, clauses 2-4, 6, 8 and 10](https://www.paddle.com/legal/terms).

With a direct processor, the buyer purchases from our business and pays through the processor; we remain the seller and own the associated invoice and applicable transaction-tax obligations. Refund and chargeback responsibilities remain subject to the processor agreement. In both models, our application must implement usage limits, permissions, credits, and subscription entitlements; that last point follows from our retained product responsibilities. [Razorpay terms, Part B, Part I](https://razorpay.com/terms/).

## Manual email preparation and tracking

- `mailto:` supports editable recipient/subject/plain-text body fields, not a portable attachment mechanism, sender-account lock, or sent callback. The user can change or abandon the prepared content. Use copyable content as a fallback. [RFC 6068](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc6068.html).
- Downloading/opening `.eml` is supported by some clients, but does not universally create an editable outgoing draft; `X-Unsent` behavior is client/version-specific. Verify each promised client path rather than promising a generic one-click Send experience. [Outlook formats](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/outlook/mail/open-eml-msg-and-oft-files-in-new-outlook-and-outlook-on-the-web), [Gmail downloads](https://support.google.com/mail/answer/9261412?hl=en).
- Gmail draft IDs and sent message IDs differ, and deleting a draft is not evidence of sending. Sent-folder placement is an observation rather than delivery proof: Gmail insert/import can label a message SENT, and Graph's send API acceptance does not prove completion or delivery. Separate user reports, mailbox observations, and delivery evidence. [Gmail drafts](https://developers.google.com/workspace/gmail/api/guides/drafts), [Gmail insert](https://developers.google.com/workspace/gmail/api/reference/rest/v1/users.messages/insert), [Graph sendMail](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/graph/api/user-sendmail?view=graph-rest-1.0).
- Creating provider-native drafts or reading replies still requires the applicable mailbox grants/verification. Gmail's compose scope also permits sending; it is not a draft-only grant. A user pressing Send does not by itself establish an exception to mailbox-data requirements. [Gmail scopes](https://developers.google.com/workspace/gmail/api/auth/scopes).
