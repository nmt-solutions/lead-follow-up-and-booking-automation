# Email eligibility and outreach suppression

The Business and shared inbox link to **Email eligibility and opt-outs**. Current Business members inspect prepared actions, independent eligibility checks, evidence and suppression history. Owners and Admins record recipient evidence and review concrete prepared content. Salespeople can record restrictive opt-outs, but cannot grant permission or publish policy. Every application command rechecks the current session, membership, role and required MFA on the protected transaction connection.

## Configure supported coverage

No production market policy or permission evidence is installed by this feature. A trusted schema-owner operator provisions the separate `platform_operator.email_policy` grant for an existing authorized user. Business roles, tax/AI/discovery grants and incoming payloads cannot grant it. Publication also requires a current MFA verification within the existing platform-operator window. The operator must have access to the Business used for the review; published policy is platform-wide, never Business-authored law.

For each supported destination country, recipient legal class and email purpose, publish a version with primary guidance URLs, reviewed rationale, accepted evidence kinds, required content checks, incoming opt-out scope, effective time and explicit review expiry. The UI accepts unknown destination/class in recipient evidence; it never enables an unknown-class policy. Buyer category, home market, publicly visible addresses, MX/domain checks, Discovery evidence, paid reach and AI text establish none of these permissions. This configurable engine does not claim to encode every jurisdiction's law or certify legal approval.

Record destination/class provenance separately from purpose-specific permission. Consent and documented relationship evidence name the policy revision reviewed, Opportunity, source reference, observed time and explicit validity. A new recipient revision can withdraw prior evidence while retaining every earlier record. Evidence reviewed against an older policy cannot automatically qualify under a changed policy. A persisted direct inquiry can qualify only for its own prepared inquiry response when the applicable policy accepts it; it does not authorize future sales follow-up or unrelated outreach. Public buying requests do not establish that relationship.

## Prepared actions and dispatch

The application derives purpose and recipient from the concrete persisted action, its exact received message, and current Opportunity association. Existing inquiry runs are `inquiry_response`; other existing due and commercial communications are conservatively `sales_follow_up`. The client cannot submit `allow`, relabel purpose, select a replacement recipient or supply eligibility to dispatch. Commercial payload hashes, original approvals, association, current ownership, current authority and queued-session checks remain authoritative.

An evaluation persists ALLOWED, BLOCKED or UNKNOWN, independent check explanations, next actions, policy/evidence references, accepted permission provenance, covered suppression IDs, sender reference, action fingerprint and evaluation time. UNKNOWN prevents dispatch. Content requirements are verified by an attributed Owner/Admin review bound to the prepared action, recipient, purpose and content fingerprint. A changed action requires a new review. There is no prepared body to review for the older generic due-action placeholder; configured content requirements therefore keep it UNKNOWN. Eligibility alone never grants first-outreach approval, commercial authority or delivery capability.

Both existing Opportunity email response/follow-up dispatch and immutable commercial email execution call `evaluateEmailAction` inside their actual final dispatch transaction. Every recipient-evidence/suppression write uses the same Business advisory lock, including the first suppression insertion. Policy publication uses an exclusive platform-policy lock; dispatch uses its shared counterpart through bounded provider I/O. The final provider boundary also fences effective-time/expiry boundaries that pass while an allowed result is being persisted. No previously allowed UI snapshot is an execution capability. Outbound adapters receive only the authoritative recipient/purpose and decision/fingerprint references, not private evidence notes.

A restriction committed before provider invocation stops stale work, including an invocation that already acquired its durable commitment. If a provider effect has already started under the lock, a racing restriction waits for that bounded attempt; it cannot undo that effect. Ambiguous committed work is not retried, and an ambiguous transport failure retains its eligibility decision. Ownership/control generations and sales history remain unchanged by suppression.

Future campaign/sequence/native-send work (#32, #34, #35, #36) must extend the concrete action-context adapter and use this same evaluator under these locks at its provider boundary. Those features and their transport activation are not implemented here. Do not substitute a standalone cached evaluation or a Resend prospecting route. Platform authentication and subscription transaction notices remain distinct server-owned flows outside this sales-action interface.

## Opt-out ingestion and scope

Supported scopes are Business-recipient outreach, recipient outreach for one Opportunity, and all sales email to that Business-recipient. Effective times are inclusive. Outreach-only scope covers sales outreach/follow-up; it does not silently suppress a genuinely separate inquiry response. All-email scope also covers that response. Scope never extends to another Business. There is no unsuppress command, speculative platform-global unsubscribe or history deletion.

Authenticated normalized imports support `opt_out`. Selected Gmail and Outlook ingestion recognizes a narrow set of explicit first-line requests such as “Unsubscribe” and “Please stop emailing me.” Quoted content and public discovery signals do not classify an opt-out. Broader or ambiguous language needs a human to record the appropriate restriction. Provider account/connection, selected processing scope, event identity, exact recipient and original received-message/native reply references must match. A missing or foreign identity cannot target another contact. Existing deferred native reply resolution handles reordered selected messages. Duplicate event/message delivery remains idempotent.

Incoming opt-outs use the configured policy's scope; without supported configuration, the conservative Business-recipient outreach stop applies. Exact native references follow the received message's current Opportunity even when one mail thread spans multiple purchasing needs. Persisted source-message fingerprints make duplicate delivery idempotent across later policy/association changes; altered replay payloads conflict rather than silently changing coverage. They do not create qualification engagement, new purchasing needs, ownership handback or notifications. The suppression record names its source and the UI provides the responsible next action. Users can inspect stored correspondence throughout.

The current supported opt-out path is a reply into the selected inbox or an authenticated Business record. There is no anonymous/public unsubscribe endpoint. A configured one-click-unsubscribe requirement remains UNKNOWN even after a content-review checkbox: no supported one-click transport capability is installed. Keep those actions blocked until the relevant native-send integration supplies and verifies that capability. Production sending remains unconfigured; no live messages or workers are activated by this issue.

## Verification and human workflow

Primary application tests use restricted real PostgreSQL, real authentication/MFA, deterministic mail/AI adapters, and the harness clock. PostgreSQL barriers exercise first insertion, evidence withdrawal and market restriction between durable commitment and provider invocation, plus in-flight effects and effective-time transitions. Native Gmail/Graph fixture ingestion verifies the actual selected-mail opt-out path. Existing immutable execution and takeover tests remain in force.

Human UI validation is pending. In a configured test Business, prepare an inquiry response or commercial action, open Email eligibility and opt-outs, and evaluate it. Missing destination/class/policy/evidence must explain UNKNOWN. An authorized policy operator can configure explicitly reviewed fixture coverage; an Owner/Admin can record the required evidence/content reviews. Reevaluate and confirm independent sender/permission/suppression checks. Record a scoped effective opt-out and dispatch covered queued work: it must be blocked while ownership, unrelated recipients/Opportunities and history remain available. Withdraw evidence or restrict policy and repeat. Check form recovery after session/MFA expiry and inspect retained revisions. Do not use real recipient outreach for acceptance.

## Primary guidance consulted 2026-11-06

- [FTC commercial-email guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business): commercial email has identity, content and opt-out obligations; B2B is not a universal exception. Purpose matters, and relationship-message categories are narrow.
- [ICO business-to-business guidance](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/direct-marketing-and-privacy-and-electronic-communications/business-to-business-marketing/?n=%40) and [electronic-mail rules](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/direct-marketing-and-privacy-and-electronic-communications/guidance-on-direct-marketing-using-electronic-mail/how-do-we-comply-with-the-pecr-electronic-mail-marketing-rules/): corporate subscribers and individual subscribers, including sole traders and relevant partnerships, require distinct treatment; public contact details do not establish consent. Direct page fetches returned 403; current indexed excerpts from these official sources were consulted. No market-specific legal rule is preconfigured from those excerpts.
- [Google sender requirements](https://support.google.com/mail/answer/81126?hl=en): receiver-provider authentication and applicable unsubscribe requirements are additional checks. A recorded review cannot manufacture a missing transport capability.

These sources motivate explicit configuration and separate facts. No statutory delay, retention period, sending rate or worldwide default permission is inferred or encoded.
