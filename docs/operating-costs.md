# Running operating cost estimate

Last verified: 2026-11-05. This ledger tracks service selections, not purchases or activated accounts. Update the estimate and its marginal change whenever a service or paid tier changes. All provider prices below are USD; account-specific contracts take precedence.

## Current priced core

The shared platform's selected fixed plan commitments are **USD 30/month**: Vercel Pro USD 20 plus Trigger.dev Hobby USD 10. Neon, excess hosting/worker usage, storage, and channel/AI consumption remain metered. These are platform-wide costs shared by the initial 1-10 Businesses, not separate subscriptions for each Business.

**Illustrative always-active core: USD 49.70-51.10/month**, before retained database history, excess usage, taxes, payment/FX charges, and the unpriced components below. This assumes one deployment, Neon at 0.25 CU for 730 hours, 1-5 GB-month of database storage, R2 within its free monthly allowances, Resend Free, and Vercel/Trigger usage within their included credits. It is a workload scenario, not a ceiling or full-product forecast; higher database compute also increases it.

For INR planning only, use a deliberately rounded **INR 100/USD conversion assumption** dated 2026-11-05; this is not a live exchange-rate quote or bank conversion. That puts the illustrated core at **INR 4,970-5,110/month**, before the same exclusions. The difference from the INR 10,000 fixed-infrastructure ceiling is not guaranteed spare budget because history, excess infrastructure usage, and other required components are still unpriced. Replace the planning rate with actual invoiced/card rates when available.

**Existing AI plan:** the operator selected their ChatGPT Pro USD 100/month plan's eligible API-credit benefit. Allocating that entire existing subscription to this application would make the same always-active core plus AI-plan cash cost **USD 149.70-151.10/month** (INR 14,970-15,110 at the planning rate), before other exclusions. This is a full-allocation scenario, not a newly requested USD 100 purchase; if the plan is shared with personal/other work, show the chosen allocation separately. The INR 10,000 infrastructure ceiling excludes the separately budgeted AI category. Eligible usage within the activated API grant can have no additional API cash charge, but neither the existing subscription nor usage beyond available credits should be represented as free operating resources.

## Payment and cash-flow accounting

- Q33 confirms INR billing for Indian Businesses and USD billing internationally. Q34 selects Razorpay; Q35 selects recurring cards first and UPI AutoPay after validation of its separate upgrade/new-mandate flow. International subscription receipts settle in INR under the documented path, so preserve invoice and settlement currencies separately. No extra processor is active by selecting a researched alternative.
- Razorpay's public subscription prices conflict: the product page advertises a temporary 0.5% surcharge, while its official pricing explainer lists 0.99%. For a cautious provisional cost scenario, use the published 0.99% surcharge until actual terms are confirmed; this is a planning assumption, not a contractual maximum or quote. FX/bank charges, account-specific terms and optional services remain additional. [Subscription product page](https://razorpay.com/subscriptions/), [official pricing explainer](https://razorpay.com/blog/razorpay-payment-gateway-pricing-explained).
- On that assumption, standard domestic 2% base + 0.99% recurring + 18% GST on fees gives **3.5282%** of the gross collection. International or domestic corporate/business 3% base + 0.99% recurring + fee GST gives **4.7082%** before FX/other charges. If the 0.5% offer is actually approved, the corresponding scenarios are 2.95% and 4.13%. Tax on the customer invoice is separate from GST charged on the processor's fees; potential input-tax recovery is not assumed as a cash saving.
- Example only: INR 100,000-equivalent gross international recurring collections at the 0.99% surcharge produce approximately **INR 4,708.20** in processing/recurring fees including fee GST, before FX/bank/refund/dispute charges. This variable cost is additional to the core infrastructure scenario; customer prices, actual payment volume and the final contract are still open.
- Standard recurring/payment charges do not add a fixed monthly subscription to the USD 30 infrastructure commitments. Quote any account/one-time activation, optional or promotional charges separately. Regular refunds retain the original processing fees/taxes; include that retained cost when estimating refunds/plan changes. [Refund FAQ](https://razorpay.com/docs/payments/refunds/faqs/).
- Direct selling replaces Paddle: its standard transaction deductions and payout assumptions are removed from the selected forecast. Razorpay pricing above is provisional, so no guaranteed saving or all-in FX cost is claimed.
- Reconcile our own gross subscription charges, collected tax, invoices/credit notes, refunds, gateway fees and their taxes, FX, settlements and bank receipts. Track tax liability and filing/remittance records separately from platform subscription revenue. Collected tax earmarked for remittance is not assumed to be platform margin.
- AI Gateway requires a credit purchase for BYOK eligibility; the minimum purchase amount was not established by the published documentation. Track the deposit as prepaid cash, then recognize actual consumption without counting both as separate operating expenses. Do not budget the free monthly AI Gateway credits after switching to its paid credit tier.
- Gateway automatically retries failed BYOK requests with its own credentials, consuming Gateway credits. A provider allowlist does not disable this. Q32 accepted fallback within a small explicitly budgeted allowance; its numeric amount remains unset. Those system-key calls cannot consume the operator's OpenAI API grant. [BYOK behavior](https://vercel.com/docs/ai-gateway/authentication-and-byok/byok).
- Pro USD 100's API grant must be activated and linked to the receiving OpenAI organization. Paid-plan age, owner/email eligibility, first-cycle proration, and cycle-end expiry affect usable funds. Use the linked organization's project API key and verify actual billing attribution; ChatGPT session allowances do not replace API credentials. [Official OpenAI API-credit benefit](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/chatgpt-api-credits).
- Gateway spend budgets exclude BYOK provider spend. Reconcile the provider's actual bill/credits and Gateway charges independently; Gateway `market_cost` is not proof of cash charged, and its `total_cost` is zero for BYOK. Do not silently add paid custom-reporting endpoints to this estimate. [Budgets](https://vercel.com/docs/ai-gateway/observability-and-spend/budgets), [reporting](https://vercel.com/docs/ai-gateway/observability-and-spend/custom-reporting).
