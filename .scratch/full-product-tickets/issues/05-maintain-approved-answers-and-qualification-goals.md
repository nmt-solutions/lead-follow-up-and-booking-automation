## What to build

A Business maintains approved knowledge, missing-information questions and booking or quotation-request goals for its sales conversations.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Create, review, update and retire approved offerings, answers and qualification requirements with an attributable history.
- [ ] Configure booking versus native RFQ goals and the Business's required qualification fields without industry-specific forks.
- [ ] Distinguish unapproved or absent information from authoritative answers; record an actionable escalation destination.
- [ ] Expose the current approved configuration to application workflows and test adapters without sharing another Business's knowledge.
- [ ] Complete the relevant persistence, application/API and UI path; verify observable state and outbound effects at the primary application seam with real internal persistence, deterministic providers and a controlled clock.
- [ ] Enforce actor/Business scope for the reads, writes, provider events and queued work touched here; validate external data and keep public errors and diagnostics safe.
- [ ] Apply the accepted discovery/handler/worker boundaries and frontend stack/design skills. After final code edits, run Prettier and lint, fix failures and report the results.
