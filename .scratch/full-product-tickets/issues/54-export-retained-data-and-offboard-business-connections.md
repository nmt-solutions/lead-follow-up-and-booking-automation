## What to build

A Business exports permitted records and offboards under explicit retention/cancellation policies without losing protected financial history or leaking credentials.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Provide authorized exports of retained prospects/evidence/conversations with contract-aware scope and immutable financial-document history.
- [ ] Manage explicit access, retention/deletion and cancellation policies; missing production policy remains visible rather than inventing legal retention periods.
- [ ] Stop/revoke affected jobs and external connections, clean up retained files/tokens through accountable retryable steps and preserve required invoice/tax evidence.
- [ ] Show number/account control/transfer and incomplete revocation status, retain the agreed records after reach reduction, and test cross-Business and signed-link isolation.
- [ ] Complete the relevant persistence, application/API and UI path; verify observable state and outbound effects at the primary application seam with real internal persistence, deterministic providers and a controlled clock.
- [ ] Enforce actor/Business scope for the reads, writes, provider events and queued work touched here; validate external data and keep public errors and diagnostics safe.
- [ ] Apply the accepted discovery/handler/worker boundaries and frontend stack/design skills. After final code edits, run Prettier and lint, fix failures and report the results.
