## What to build

Each Business saves what it sells, whom it targets and where it operates without an industry or India-first restriction.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Capture specifications, minimum quantities, delivery/service constraints, reviewed buyer categories and exclusions.
- [ ] Suggest geography from supplied address information but require confirmation of the Home market; keep selected additional markets explicit.
- [ ] Represent organizational buyers, sole proprietors, business end users and resellers accurately; personal-use consumer targeting requires an explicit buying request.
- [ ] Demonstrate different steel-supplier and scissors-business configurations through the same UI, commands and persistence.
- [ ] Complete the relevant persistence, application/API and UI path; verify observable state and outbound effects at the primary application seam with real internal persistence, deterministic providers and a controlled clock.
- [ ] Enforce actor/Business scope for the reads, writes, provider events and queued work touched here; validate external data and keep public errors and diagnostics safe.
- [ ] Apply the accepted discovery/handler/worker boundaries and frontend stack/design skills. After final code edits, run Prettier and lint, fix failures and report the results.
