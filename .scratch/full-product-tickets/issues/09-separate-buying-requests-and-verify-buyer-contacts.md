## What to build

Salespeople distinguish Potential buyers from genuinely open buying requests and can inspect contact-role evidence and uncertainty.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Show Potential buyers and Open buying requests separately, with independent counts and explicit dated/current-open evidence.
- [ ] Keep expired, undated and uncertain requests visible with their actual status; include personal-use consumers only with their supporting explicit public request.
- [ ] Classify Buyer contact, General business contact and missing contact; require evidence for a purchasing role or owner rather than assuming any employee is a buyer.
- [ ] Retain strong matches with general/no contacts; keep evidence-backed, contactable and named-buyer counts distinct and preserve provenance into later workflows.
- [ ] Complete the relevant persistence, application/API and UI path; verify observable state and outbound effects at the primary application seam with real internal persistence, deterministic providers and a controlled clock.
- [ ] Enforce actor/Business scope for the reads, writes, provider events and queued work touched here; validate external data and keep public errors and diagnostics safe.
- [ ] Apply the accepted discovery/handler/worker boundaries and frontend stack/design skills. After final code edits, run Prettier and lint, fix failures and report the results.
