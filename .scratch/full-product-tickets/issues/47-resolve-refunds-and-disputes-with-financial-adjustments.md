## What to build

An operator processes a full/partial refund or dispute and the Business sees linked payment, document and subscription consequences.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Provide authorized review/action/status flows linked to the original payment and invoice, with retained evidence and customer notification effects.
- [ ] Reconcile authoritative refund/dispute results, preserve pending/ambiguous states and prevent duplicate refunds or repeated entitlement changes.
- [ ] Create the applicable credit-note/tax adjustments and apply explicitly configured subscription/access policy without rewriting financial history.
- [ ] Support deterministic partial-refund and dispute recovery scenarios plus gateway contract checks for enabled actions.
- [ ] Complete the relevant persistence, application/API and UI path; verify observable state and outbound effects at the primary application seam with real internal persistence, deterministic providers and a controlled clock.
- [ ] Enforce actor/Business scope for the reads, writes, provider events and queued work touched here; validate external data and keep public errors and diagnostics safe.
- [ ] Apply the accepted discovery/handler/worker boundaries and frontend stack/design skills. After final code edits, run Prettier and lint, fix failures and report the results.
