# Prospect Discovery and Sales Automation

Shared terms for businesses evaluating and subscribing to the service across industries.

## Language

### Participants and purchasing

**Business**:
An organization evaluating or paying for the service for its own customer acquisition. A business may operate in any industry.
_Avoid_: Studio as the universal customer name, workspace, account

**Business buyer**:
A buyer purchasing for business use or resale, including a sole proprietor, service provider, retailer, or larger organization.

**Individual consumer**:
A person purchasing goods or services for personal use.

**Prospect**:
A person or organization identified as a potential buyer of a business's products or services.

**Engaged lead**:
A prospect who has directly inquired with the business or replied to it with buying interest. A public buying signal alone does not establish this engagement.

**Opportunity**:
One distinct purchasing need being pursued with a buyer. A buyer may have multiple opportunities; repeated messages about the same need belong to the same opportunity.

### Discovery and reach

**Discovery brief**:
A business's description of what it sells and which buyers it wants to reach, including product requirements, buyer categories, geography, and exclusions.

**Prospect discovery**:
Finding potential customers and relevant publicly listed business contacts using public information on the internet.

**Saved search**:
A reusable discovery brief that a business can run on demand or on a recurring schedule.

**Discovery run**:
One execution of a discovery brief to find relevant prospects and buying requests.

**Discovery budget**:
The resource or credit allowance a business has authorized for one discovery run.

**Geographic reach**:
The geographic markets included in a business's prospect discovery coverage.

**Home market**:
A business's confirmed operating or service geography, used to prioritize discovery by default.

**Evidence-backed match**:
A prospect with public evidence supporting its fit with a discovery brief, whether or not a usable contact route has been found.

**Contactable prospect**:
A prospect with a usable publicly listed contact route. Contact availability alone does not establish permission or approval to send a message.

**Buyer contact**:
Publicly listed contact details for a person responsible for a prospect's purchasing or supplier-selection decisions, such as a procurement head or business owner.

**General business contact**:
A publicly listed business mailbox, phone number, or contact form without a confirmed connection to the person responsible for purchasing.

**Buying signal**:
Public evidence suggesting that a prospect may currently be seeking a relevant product or service, distinct from general suitability as a customer.

**Open buying request**:
An explicit, dated public request for a relevant product or service that its source still shows as open. A request with uncertain status is not established as open.

### Conversations and channels

**Incoming inquiry**:
A message received by a business from someone asking about its products or services.

**Inquiry handling**:
Responding to and managing inquiries a business receives, including qualification, follow-up, and arranging the next sales step.

**Connected mailbox**:
A business-controlled email inbox linked to the service for sending and synchronizing sales conversations.

**Mailbox scope**:
The sales mailboxes or conversations a business has selected for the service to process, including any selected historical correspondence.

**Email handoff**:
A prepared email that a person opens in their own email application, reviews, and chooses whether to send. Preparing or opening it does not establish that it was sent.

**Verified sending domain**:
An email domain that a business has demonstrated control over and authorized the service to use for sending.

**Service-provided address**:
A business-specific email address on a domain operated by the service.

**Outreach sender**:
A dedicated email identity a business uses to initiate conversations with discovered prospects.

**Sending identity**:
A Business-owned email identity with a stated address origin, dedicated outreach or ordinary sales purpose, and explicit intended uses.

**Ordinary sales identity**:
An email identity used for a Business's incoming inquiries and established sales conversations, separate from its dedicated Outreach sender.

**Reply route**:
The explicit reply-to address and selected sales inbox through which replies reach the Business's conversation workflow.

**Sender health**:
The authentication and delivery condition of an email sender, assessed through delivery failures and recipient complaints.

**Email eligibility**:
Whether a particular email may be sent to a recipient under the applicable destination, recipient-type, permission, sender, and opt-out conditions. Paid geographic reach alone does not establish eligibility.

**WhatsApp business identity**:
The customer-controlled business identity and associated number used for WhatsApp conversations with prospects and engaged leads.

**Call activity**:
A log of a human sales call, including its outcome, written notes, and next action.

### Authority and actions

**Commercial authority**:
The permissions a business grants the service to communicate prices, issue quotations, offer discounts, or accept orders on its behalf. Each subscribing business determines its own permissions.

**Conversion goal**:
The sales outcome a business wants an engaged lead to reach, such as a confirmed appointment or a qualified quotation request.

**First-outreach approval**:
The business's approval of the recipients, initial message, and bounded follow-up sequence before contacting newly discovered prospects.

**Follow-up sequence**:
An approved series of messages with defined timing, maximum attempts, and stop conditions for an unanswered outreach attempt.

**Human takeover**:
A person assuming control of an opportunity while its automated messages are paused across channels until control is explicitly returned.

### Pilot and subscription

**Direct selling**:
The service operator sells platform subscriptions to Businesses in its own name, retaining the sale's invoicing, payment, and tax responsibilities while a payment gateway processes collections.

**Billing profile**:
A Business's legal identity, billing address, invoice contact, and applicable tax identifiers and supporting evidence for its platform subscription.

**Billing currency**:
The currency in which a Business's platform subscription is priced, invoiced, and charged.

**Settlement currency**:
The currency in which a payment processor pays subscription receipts to the service operator's bank account.

**Tax treatment**:
The documented tax rules and evidence applied to a subscription transaction, including any applicable tax, exemption, reverse charge, or export treatment.

**Tax obligation**:
A registration, reporting, filing, or payment requirement associated with the service operator's subscription sales in a jurisdiction, with its status and supporting records.

**Credit note**:
A document recording an adjustment to an issued invoice, including the corresponding value and tax changes.

**Global budget**:
The service operator's cross-provider spending budget for running the platform. It is separate from each business's Discovery budgets and excludes charges billed directly to a Business by its channel providers.

**Customer-paid channel charge**:
A charge a Business pays directly to an external communication provider for its own account, separately from its platform subscription.

**Merchant of Record**:
The legal seller or reseller of a subscription transaction that invoices the buyer and handles the contracted payment and applicable transaction-tax responsibilities before paying proceeds to the service operator.

**Paid pilot**:
A bounded, paid evaluation of the service by one business before deciding whether to continue on a monthly subscription.
_Avoid_: Free trial

**Renewal**:
A business continuing on a paid monthly subscription after completing its paid pilot, at a monthly price agreed before the pilot starts.
